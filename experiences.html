Intimate Celebrations

Mark Your Milestones in Our Sun-Baked Sanctuary

The Terracotta Room offers an elegant, raw-textured backdrop for small-scale events that demand true presence. We design custom programs around your most cherished celebrations.

  • Elopements & Promises: A private, minimalist ceremony held under the ancient olive boughs, framed by terracotta archways.
  • Anniversaries & Renewals: Candlelit dinners on the terrace, customized floral arrangements, and sunrise wellness sessions.
