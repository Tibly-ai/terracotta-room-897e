Architectural Philosophy

Crafted from the Earth, Curved for the Sun

The architecture of The Terracotta Room is inspired by the simple, enduring farmhouses of the Southern European coastline. Plaster walls were hand-layered to capture the shifting shadows of the oak trees outside, creating a gentle interior glow that softens as daylight fades.

Every archway and opening has been placed to draw in the coastal breeze, minimizing the boundary between the internal lounge and the central courtyard. We chose authentic materials that wear their history visibly: reclaimed timber beams, raw limestone flooring, and hand-formed clay tiles that cool underfoot during the peak of midday heat.

We curate our spaces with objects that have lived. From antique stone vessels sourced from Mediterranean villages to hand-woven linen drapery that filters the golden light, the house is a celebration of imperfection, warmth, and the beauty of slow living.

House Details & Policies

What are the arrival and departure hours?

Arrive anytime after 4:00 PM on your scheduled day. Departure is strictly by 11:00 AM so we can thoroughly prepare the plaster rooms and courtyard garden spaces for the next arriving party.

Can we host an intimate gathering during our stay?

We welcome quiet afternoon long lunches and peaceful dinners with friends. However, commercial events, professional photoshoots, or parties exceeding our maximum capacity of 8 overnight guests require written approval from our host before booking.

How do you handle amenities and property care?

Our groundskeeper discreetly maintains the central plunge pool, stone hot tub, and garden plants every second morning around 7:00 AM, ensuring absolute privacy for your mornings. Clean towels, pool sheets, and custom olive-oil soap bars are provided.

What is your cancellation and booking policy?

Because we host only one group at a time on our private estate, cancellations made up to 30 days prior to your arrival date will receive a full refund. Cancellations between 14 and 30 days before arrival will receive a 50% credit for a future stay.
